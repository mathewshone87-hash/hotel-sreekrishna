OUR STORY

Welcome to Hotel Sreekrishna

Hotel Sreekrishna is a casual and economical eatery located in Kavumbhakam, Pooppara, Kerala (685619). We serve traditional Kerala-style village foods, tasty breakfast items and a variety of meals for families, travellers and food lovers.

From soft appam and puttu to Kerala meals, biriyani, chicken specialities and refreshing drinks, there's something for everyone to enjoy.
